Let yourself melt

Let Yourself Melt is a mixtape cover created from a personal selection of songs I listen to. I translated the overall mood and energy of the music into a single visual composition, combining a hand-painted acrylic artwork with the physical format of a vinyl record. The artwork was painted traditionally by hand and then adapted digitally into the final cover design.
